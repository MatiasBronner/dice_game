Rolling Die Maze

Study links

Send each participant one link

Links 2–4 are the training conditions, link 5 is the delayed test, and link 1 is for testing the game yourself.

The link decides the condition. Paths are numbered so participants can't tell which group they're in. Add ?pid=ID to fill in the participant ID automatically (Prolific's PROLIFIC_PID also works).

Session 1 (links 2–4): practice, 30 training levels (10 easy, 10 medium, 10 hard) with that group's assistance, then 6 levels on their own (immediate test) and a 6-question rule quiz.
Session 2 (link 5): 6 new levels with no help for anyone (delayed test, matched in difficulty to the immediate test), then the quiz. Participants must use the same ID in both sessions.
Data goes to Supabase: sessions, level_results, quiz_answers, events, participants. Use the Table Editor to view and export it. If a participant reloads the page, they can pick up at the level they were on.
