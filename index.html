Study links

Send each participant one link

Links 2–4 are the study conditions. Link 1 is for testing the game yourself.

The link decides the condition. Paths are numbered so participants can't tell which group they're in. Participants sign in with their first and last name, so everyone they play is linked to the same person, however many times you test them.

Each session (links 2–4): practice, 30 training levels (10 easy, 10 medium, 10 hard) with that group's assistance, then 6 levels on their own and a 6-question rule quiz. To test someone again, send them the same link; their new session is linked to their earlier ones by name.
Data goes to Supabase: participants (one row per person, with their name), sessions, level_results, quiz_answers, events. Use the Table Editor to view and export it. If a participant reloads the page, they can pick up at the level they were on.
